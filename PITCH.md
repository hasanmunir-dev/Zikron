# Khazanix

## The Problem

People save information everywhere:

* WhatsApp
* Google Drive
* Screenshots
* Downloads
* Notes Apps
* Emails

Later, they remember the information exists but cannot find it.

---

## The Solution

Khazanix is a personal second-brain platform that combines:

* Notes
* Documents
* Images
* Reminders
* Tasks
* Study Materials
* Knowledge Management

inside one searchable system.

---

## Key Features

* Fast Capture
* Smart Search
* Categories & Tags
* Google Drive Integration
* Reminders
* Browser Notifications
* AI Search
* OCR
* Chrome Extension

---

## Target Users

* Students
* Developers
* Researchers
* Freelancers
* Professionals
* Lifelong Learners

---

## Vision

A future where users never ask:

"Where did I save this?"

Instead they simply search and instantly find it.

---

## Tagline

**Capture Everything. Find Anything. Forget Nothing.**
